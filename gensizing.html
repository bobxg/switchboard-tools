Site Conditions & Derating

Site derating: Generator output is derated for altitude and high ambient temperature. This tool applies typical values (3.5% per 300 m above 1000 m, 2% per 5.5°C above 40°C) — use the manufacturer’s derating curves for final selection (AS/NZS 3010, ISO 8528-1).
