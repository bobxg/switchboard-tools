    Cubicle sizes (mm)

    Defaults follow the Form of separation chosen under Incomer, for 3-pole fixed MCCBs. The Form 3b 250 A and 400 A heights assume the breaker is mounted on its side (horizontal); use 300 for a 4-pole or withdrawable 250 A unit. Tier heights vary between switchboard systems — set them to your builder’s module sizes. Feeder cubicles are 600 wide; the incoming section follows the main switch rating.
