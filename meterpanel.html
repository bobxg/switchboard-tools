Meter Panel Sizer

Size a multi-occupancy whole-current (direct-connected, ≤ 80 A per phase) meter panel for NSW developments. Rules are taken from the Service and Installation Rules of NSW, April 2026 and the Metering Annexure, July 2018, which the Rules adopt as the Metering Installation Requirements (S&IR cl. 1.9.26). Neither sets meter position size, meters per column or cubicle dimensions: those values are typical only and must be confirmed with the Metering Coordinator / Metering Provider and the enclosure manufacturer.
