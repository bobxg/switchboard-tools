Design Notes

Busbar design considerations per AS/NZS 61439:
  • Temperature rise: Max 70°C for tin-plated joints, 90°C for silver-plated joints, 105°C for insulated bars (above 40°C ambient)
  • Short-circuit: Busbar must withstand Icw (rated short-time withstand current) for rated duration. Adiabatic withstand I = k·S/√t with k = 226√ln((234.5+θf)/(234.5+θi)) for Cu (148 and 228 for Al), θi = operating temperature, θf = 200°C — about 120–135 A/mm² for Cu over 1 s
  • Electrodynamic forces: Peak force on the middle phase F = 0.866 × 0.2 × ip² × L / a (N, ip in kA, ip = n×Ik with n from AS/NZS 61439.1 Table 7). Bending stress σ = 0.73·F·L/(8Z) must not exceed 1.5×Rp0.2 (IEC 60865-1).
  • Voltage drop: Busbar voltage drop = I × (R×cos φ + X×sin φ) × L. Typically negligible for short bus lengths
  • Earth bar: Size the PE bar to AS/NZS 61439.1 cl. 8.4.3 (S/2 as a typical minimum for large phase bars) and for the earth-fault withstand
  • Neutral bar: Full-size neutral for harmonic-rich loads (3rd harmonic). Can be 50% of phase for linear balanced loads
