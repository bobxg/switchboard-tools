Method

  • Current rating Iz = Itab × ktemp × kgroup × parallel runs; requires Iz ≥ IB.
  • Temperature factor k = √((θc − θa) / (θc − θref)), θref = 30°C air / 20°C ground.
  • Voltage drop uses impedance at operating temperature: Vd = k × I × L × (R cosφ + X sinφ), k = 2 (1-ph) or √3 (3-ph). Conductor R from IEC 60228 at 20°C, corrected to 75°C.
  • Short-circuit check (adiabatic): S ≥ I√t / k with k = 143 (Cu X-90), 115 (Cu V-90), 94 (Al X-90), 76 (Al V-90).
  • Tabulated ratings are indicative — they follow the IEC 60364-5-52 reference methods (three loaded conductors). Confirm the final selection against the current-rating tables of AS/NZS 3008.1.1 for the actual cable type and installation condition.
